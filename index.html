Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is included in Sanchar Infratech's Project Management Consultancy (PMC)?

A: PMC services include pre-construction feasibility, architectural blueprint auditing, 3D BIM modeling, cost estimation, quality assurance checks, 24/7 live site camera surveillance, contractor oversight, and regulatory compliance management.

A: Yes, we implement Indian Green Building Council (IGBC) certified standards, utilizing carbon-neutral construction practices, solar energy integration, energy-efficient MEP utility systems, and eco-conscious building materials.

A: Timelines depend on project scope: commercial and residential high-rise builds typically range from 12 to 24 months, while custom architectural design and Vastu planning consults are completed in 2 to 6 weeks.
